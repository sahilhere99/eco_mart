Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not satisfied, you can return your item(s) within 30 days of delivery.

Eligibility for Returns

  • Items must be in their original condition with all tags attached.
  • Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be accompanied by a valid receipt or proof of purchase.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our customer service team to obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
  2. Package the item(s) securely in their original packaging, if possible.
  3. Ship the item(s) back to us at the address provided by our customer service team.

Refunds and Exchanges

Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item(s). Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment.

Exchanges are only available for items of equal or lesser value. Please contact our customer service team to initiate an exchange.

Exceptions

The following items are not eligible for returns or exchanges:

  • Final sale items
  • Personalized or customized items
  • Items that are damaged or worn

If you have any questions or concerns about our return policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
