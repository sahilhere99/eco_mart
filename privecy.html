Privacy Notice

Last updated: [30-june-2024]

Ecomart is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a responsible and secure manner. We collect personal information that you provide to us, such as:

  • Name and contact information, such as email address, phone number, and physical address
  • Login credentials, such as username and password
  • Payment information, such as credit card numbers and billing addresses
  • Information about your device, such as IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Information about your interactions with our website and mobile application, such as browsing history and search queries

How do we use your personal information?

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide you with our products and services
  • To process your transactions and maintain your account
  • To communicate with you about our products and services
  • To personalize your experience on our website and mobile application

  • To detect and prevent fraud and other illegal activities

We share your personal information with the following parties:

  • Law enforcement agencies and regulatory bodies
  • Third-party advertisers and analytics providers

How do we protect your personal information?

We take the security of your personal information seriously and have implemented various measures to protect it, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive information
  • Firewalls and intrusion detection systems
  • Secure servers and data centers
  • Access controls and authentication protocols

What are your rights?

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • The right to access and correct your personal information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal information
  • The right to request a copy of your personal information in a portable format
